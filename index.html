Internet Speed Requirements for IPTV

A stable internet connection is important for smooth streaming. Actual requirements depend on the application, video quality, network conditions, and number of connected devices.

  • SD Streaming: Around 3–5 Mbps.
  • HD Streaming: Around 5–10 Mbps.
  • Full HD Streaming: Around 10–15 Mbps or more.
  • 4K Streaming: Around 25 Mbps or more.
